## About

Building secure, resilient, and cost-efficient AI & Data Platforms at scale.

I help organizations design and evolve cloud-native platforms that reduce cloud costs,
eliminate operational friction, strengthen security and governance, and enable engineering
teams to deliver AI and data products faster.

I believe great platforms are built on timeless engineering principles. My focus is long-term
maintainability, operational excellence, clear governance, thoughtful documentation, and
avoiding unnecessary complexity.

Recently, I led the evolution of a data platform that reduced AWS infrastructure costs by 25%+,
achieved zero production incidents, and enabled Data teams to accelerate delivery through
self-service platform capabilities, resilient architecture, comprehensive observability, and
security by default.

I'm a strong advocate for open source — not simply to reduce licensing costs, but to build
flexible, vendor-neutral platforms with an exceptional long-term cost-benefit ratio.

### Core Concepts & Architectural Domains

- **Platform Engineering & DevEx** — IDP, Progressive Delivery, GitOps, IaC, Service Mesh.
- **SRE & FinOps** — SLI, SLO, SLA, Tracing, Chaos Engineering, Incident Response &
  Post-Mortems, HA & Disaster Recovery, Cloud Cost Optimization, Capacity Planning.
- **DevSecOps & Security** — Security by Design, Zero-Trust Architecture, Policy as Code,
  Shift-Left Security, Compliance.
- **DataOps, MLOps & Streaming** — Data Lakehouse Architecture, Event-Driven Architecture,
  Stream Processing, CDC, Data Mesh, Data Lineage, Data Governance & Contracts.
